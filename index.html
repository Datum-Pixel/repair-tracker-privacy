Repair Tracker — Field Edition

Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 3, 2026

Overview

Repair Tracker — Field Edition is an offline-first Android app for documenting concrete repairs, measurements, rebar, field notes, and production progress. The developer is DatumTrail. Privacy inquiries: invictusmiel@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information you enter, including project, building, and area names; drawings and dimensions; repair quantities; production tasks; rebar details; notes; and numbered references. This may include personal or confidential information if you choose to enter it. Records are used to provide the app's field-documentation features.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not require an account, display advertisements, or use developer-operated cloud synchronization. Project records are stored locally and are not automatically sent to developer-operated servers. The developer does not automatically receive your project records. If you choose to export or share a backup with another app, cloud storage provider, employer, or person, that recipient may process the information under its own practices.

Support communications

If you email the developer, the developer receives the email address, message, and any information or files you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request or provide support and is kept as needed for those purposes. You may contact the developer at the email address below to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Storage and backup

Project records are stored in application storage on your device. Repair Tracker provides manual export and restore of JSON backups. Exported backups are not encrypted by the app and should be treated as potentially sensitive. This release is configured to exclude app-owned data from standard Android automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer where supported. Operating-system or device-provider behavior may differ, and files you deliberately save outside the app may be included in backups managed by that destination.

Retention and deletion

App records remain in local storage until deleted through the app or device controls. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app generally removes app-private local records. Backup files you exported outside the app must be deleted separately from their saved locations. There is no developer-hosted account to delete.

Security

The app uses Android application storage and user-selected file access for backup operations. It does not encrypt exported JSON backup files. Protect your device with a screen lock and securely store or transfer exported backups. No local storage or transfer process is completely risk-free.

Children

Repair Tracker is intended for construction professionals aged 18 and over, not children.

Policy changes

This policy may change if app functionality or data practices change. The updated policy and effective date will be published on the public privacy-policy webpage.
