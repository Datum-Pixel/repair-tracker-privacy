Repair Tracker - Field Edition

Privacy Policy Effective date: November 4, 2026

Overview

Repair Tracker - Field Edition is an offline-first Android application for documenting concrete repairs, measurements, rebar, field notes, and production progress.

The developer is DatumTrail. Privacy inquiries may be directed to invictusmiel@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information you enter, including project, building, and area names; drawings and dimensions; repair quantities; production tasks; rebar details; notes; and numbered references.

This may include personal or confidential information if you choose to enter it. Records are used to provide the app's field-documentation features.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not require an account, display advertisements, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or use developer-operated cloud synchronization.

Project records are stored locally and are not automatically transmitted to developer-operated servers. The developer does not automatically receive your project records.

If you choose to export or share a backup with another application, cloud storage provider, employer, or person, that recipient may process the information according to its own practices.

Support communications

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, your email address, message, and any attachments you send will be received for the purpose of responding to your inquiry or providing support.

Support correspondence is retained as needed for those purposes. You may request deletion by contacting the developer, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Storage and backup

Project records are stored in application storage on your device.

Repair Tracker provides manual export and restoration of JSON backups. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the application and should be treated as potentially sensitive.

This release is configured to exclude app-owned data from standard Android automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer where supported.

Operating system and device-provider behavior may differ. Files deliberately saved outside the application may be included in backups managed by those destinations.

Retention and deletion

Application records remain in local storage until deleted through the application or device controls.

Clearing application storage or uninstalling the application generally removes app-private local records.

Exported backups stored outside the application must be deleted separately. There is no developer-hosted account to delete.

Security

The application uses Android application storage and user-selected file access for backup operations.

Exported JSON backup files are not encrypted by the application. Users should protect their devices with screen locks and securely store or transfer exported backups.

No local storage or transfer process is completely risk-free.

Children

Repair Tracker - Field Edition is designed for construction professionals, students, and individuals involved in field documentation. The application is not specifically designed or marketed for children.

Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if application functionality or data practices change.

The revised policy and its effective date will be published on the public Privacy Policy website.
